## Choose your role

- [Users](/docs/en/user/account-and-security/): account security and notifications, Minecraft identities, community content, reports and appeals, orders, and payments.
- [Staff](/docs/en/staff/workspace/): moderation queues, cases, report appeals, and evidence.
- [Administrators](/docs/en/admin/roles-permissions-settings/): roles, permissions, settings, the website CMS, content recovery, applications, and plugins.
- [Operators](/docs/en/operations/backup-release-rollback/): deployment, site and world backups, Hostd, nodes, jobs, metrics, and payment providers.
- Plugin developers: Developer Mode, the SDK, REST API, and marketplace operations.

English pages are translated progressively. If an English page is missing, Starlight displays the Simplified Chinese source with a fallback notice instead of silently presenting it as a completed translation.

Use the documentation shipped with your installed CE release. Available operations also depend on enabled features, permissions, and configured services; EE and EE-PVP workflows do not become available through these instructions.
