## Choose your role

- Users: account security, Minecraft identities, community content, orders, and payments.
- Staff: moderation queues, cases, reports, and evidence.
- Administrators: roles, permissions, settings, the website CMS, applications, and plugins.
- Operators: deployment, backups, Hostd, nodes, jobs, metrics, and payment providers.
- Plugin developers: Developer Mode, the SDK, REST API, and marketplace operations.
