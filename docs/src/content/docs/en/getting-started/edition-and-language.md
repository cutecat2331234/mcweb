This site belongs to **McWeb Community Edition (CE)**. It covers the shared platform: accounts, community, commerce, Minecraft integration, the website CMS, staff moderation, operations, and trusted plugins.

EE real-time channels and enterprise collaboration are outside this edition. EE-PVP applications, testing, Tier, ranking, and season workflows are also outside this edition.

Simplified Chinese is the default language at `/docs/`. English is published progressively under `/docs/en/`. Missing English translations fall back to the Simplified Chinese source with a visible notice.
